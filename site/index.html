a pointing hand for your feeds

manicule ☞

Below is what's new across seventeen deliberately mismatched feeds — code, science, essays, podcasts, sports, food, games. Point at a few things you'd actually read. Wave off a couple you wouldn't. Watch everything else reorder. Nothing is stored anywhere; your marks live in this page's address, so the link is your taste.

how it works, in full

Every entry's headline and blurb was turned into a 384-number vector once, on the server that built this page (a small local model, no API). Your browser averages the vectors of what you marked into one kept vector, and what you dismissed into one dismissed vector. Then each entry is scored:

score = cos(entry, kept) − λ · cos(entry, dismissed)

That's the whole algorithm. No training, no history, no model of you — two averages and a subtraction. λ keeps a dismissal a nudge rather than a veto; drag it to feel the difference. Each score is shown decomposed, with the kept item it most resembles, so you can always see why something is where it is. Until you mark something there is no taste to rank by, so the list stays newest-first.
