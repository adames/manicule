how it works

every entry turns into a vector and your browser averages the vectors you marked into one kept vector, or one you dismissed into one dismissed vector. then we score it

score=cos(entry, kept)−λ · cos(entry, dismissed) what you pointed at what you waved off × λ

    Same math, one Python file; your marks come from any folder of notes:
